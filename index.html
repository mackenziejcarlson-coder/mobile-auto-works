Privacy Policy

Mobile Auto Works LLC

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Mobile Auto Works LLC (“Mobile Auto Works,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, request mobile automotive services, interact with our social media pages, or respond to our advertisements.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, email address, and service location.
  • Service and vehicle information: vehicle year, make, model, symptoms or repair requests, appointment details, and information you choose to provide about your vehicle.
  • Communications: information in calls, voicemails, emails, texts, website inquiries, or messages sent through social media.
  • Transaction information: details needed to prepare estimates, provide services, issue invoices, and process payments. Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers.
  • Online and advertising information: information such as browser or device data, approximate location, pages visited, ad interactions, and cookie or similar technology data, where available through our website or service providers.

Please do not send payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email, social media messages, or text messages.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to questions and requests for estimates or service.
  • Schedule appointments, travel to service locations, diagnose reported issues, and provide automotive services.
  • Communicate about appointments, estimates, repairs, invoices, and customer support.
  • Process payments, maintain business records, and manage our business.
  • Measure and improve our website, advertising, and customer experience.
  • Prevent fraud, protect safety and property, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Send promotional communications only where permitted by law and, when required, with your consent. You can ask us to stop promotional communications at any time.

3. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Our website or the platforms that support it may use cookies, pixels, analytics, or similar technologies to operate the site, understand visits, and measure advertising performance. If you interact with our Facebook or other social media advertisements, the platform may collect information about that interaction under its own privacy policy and settings.

We may use advertising and reporting services, including Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to understand whether advertisements are reaching people and generating inquiries. Depending on the service and settings, these providers may receive or collect online identifiers, device or browser information, and activity related to an advertisement or website visit. We do not control the privacy practices of these providers. Review their policies and account settings for details and available choices.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features or measurement.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information as reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers: companies that help us host our website, manage business communications, schedule work, process payments, or maintain records.
  • Advertising and analytics providers: platforms that help deliver, measure, or report on advertising, subject to their tools and settings.
  • Professional advisers or authorities: when needed for legal, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.
  • Business transfers: if our business or its assets are reorganized, transferred, or sold, subject to applicable law.

We may also disclose information when you direct us to do so or when disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by law. Some privacy laws define certain advertising-related disclosures as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” even when no money changes hands; your available rights depend on the law that applies to you.

5. Phone Calls, Texts, and Messages

If you call, text, email, or message us, we use the information you provide to respond and handle your request. Your mobile carrier may charge standard messaging or data rates. We do not promise that every message or call will be received or retained. If we introduce automated or recurring marketing messages in the future, we will obtain any consent required by law and provide applicable opt-out instructions.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and tax records, handling disputes, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods vary based on the type of information and the circumstances.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no website, email, messaging service, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask us to delete information where we are able to do so. We may need to retain certain information for legal, accounting, service, safety, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Depending on where you live and whether applicable legal requirements are met, you may have additional rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of certain personal information, or to opt out of certain targeted advertising or data processing. To make a request, use the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You can also limit ad personalization through the settings provided by the relevant social media or advertising platform and manage cookies through your browser.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services and website are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website, social media pages, or communications may link to third-party websites or services. Those third parties operate under their own terms and privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. This policy does not cover their independent practices.
